# Field guide: where to go, and when

Read the strip at the top of your screen. Its colour is the Keeper's mood, and the
mood decides what is worth doing for the next six hours. Everything below is also
in the game, under **H**.

---

## ☀ Bright — gold is falling

**Hunt the named zones.** The chip names where the gold fell; the map marks them.
Chests fall in every mood, but Bright is the mood where the Keeper adds new gold
on top of what the Hoard puts out — this is the richest hunting of the four.
Chests get bigger the further down you go — a deep chest is worth about twenty
surface chests — and the shallow, ungated ring takes the largest share of the
epoch's gold, so the surface is worth walking again.

**Bank what you carry.** Carried gold is not yours until you bank it at a vault.
Luméira's plaza has the one everybody starts at, and every zone keeps one of its
own, usually near the way in.

**Dig up your tombstones.** At the Crypt in Hallowmere. The Hoard pays them back,
with a premium when gold is dear. This is the only mood in which the Crypt pays
out.

**Spend, if you were going to.** Old Bessany's Store. Every second purchase burns
half its price, which pushes the peg the right way.

---

## ○ Quiet — the Hoard stocks the floors

**Hunt anyway.** Nothing new is minted in a Quiet epoch, but chests still fall.
The Hoard puts back what the world swallowed — chests nobody found, bodies nobody
came back for — and hides it again. Nothing bites while the world is Quiet, so
this is the safest hunting, and the safest carrying, you will get.

**Sweep up what others missed.** Chests from earlier turns are still lying there,
and they crumble to the Hoard after four epochs. Somebody else's careless
afternoon is your quiet one.

**Craft.** Dust into potions, hide into cloaks, wisp-light into lanterns, at any
cauldron.

**Bind torches.** At the Masonry in Lanternfall, for health and the deep doors.
Bind them now; unbinding takes six epochs and you will want them before then.

**Plant or uproot.** Brannock's plots in Hallowmere. Claim what has grown.

**Feed the flock, hatch eggs, repair, run errands.** The Farmsteads, the Store,
the Keeper's Board. Sponsors' chests turn up in Quiet epochs as well.

---

## ☾ Dark — the Crypt opens

**Carry less, bank sooner.** The skeletons bite: a twentieth of what you are
carrying, every time, and it goes to the Hoard.

**Chests still fall — keep the trips short.** The Hoard stocks the floors in every
mood, Dark included. The gold is there; what changes is that holding it costs you.
Shallow runs, and a vault between each one.

**Bury gold for tombstones.** One for one, at the Crypt down the Procession Way.
You are buying the recovery — they pay out when the world turns Bright, and the
gold that pays you is the gold other people lost in the dark.

**Stay out of the deep** — unless you are hunting monsters rather than gold. Town,
the Farmsteads and the guilds are the safe shift.

## Always true

- **Chests fall every epoch, in every mood.** What the mood changes is how much
  new gold the Keeper puts in on top of the Hoard's, and how expensive it is to
  carry what you find.
- **Four epochs is a day.** An unfound chest, or your body with gold on it,
  crumbles to the Hoard after four. If you fell, get back within a day.
- **Unbinding takes six epochs.** Bind torches before you need them, not when.
- **The doors want torches bound, not spent:** ten for the Drowned Chapel, fifty
  for the Bone Halls, two hundred for the Deep Vault. Past two hundred they still
  open doors, but add no more health.
- **Six hours to a turn**, four turns a day. The strip counts down to the next one
  and rings a bell when it comes.
- **Every zone has a vault, usually near the way in.** Until you reach one, everything you hold is carrying.
