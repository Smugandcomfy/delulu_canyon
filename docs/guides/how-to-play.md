# How to play

A shipwrecked cove, a lantern-lit town, and the dungeons beneath. Gold appears
in the world every few hours — real gold you can keep — and it's yours if you
go and find it.

The game runs at **[delulu.game](https://delulu.game)**. This guide is also
in the game itself, under **H**. The [field guide](field-guide.md) is the short
version of *when* to do things; the [quest guide](quests.md) is the long list of
things to do and the people who will ask you to do them.

## Your first ten minutes

1. **Log in** with Internet Identity, choose a name, choose a face. You wash up
   at the harbour of Luméira.
2. **Click anywhere to walk.** Arrow keys or WASD step one tile. Q and E turn
   the camera. The minimap in the corner fills in as you explore; click it for
   the whole world.
3. **Talk to people.** Marrow Quill at the harbour, Old Isolde by the lanterns,
   Brannock in the graveyard — walk up and press Space. They'll tell you how
   things work here.
4. **Find a chest.** Chests appear when you're a few tiles away, and a small
   chest waits near the shore for every newcomer. Open it and the gold is yours
   to carry.
5. **Bank it.** Walk to the vault — the pedestal near where you arrived — and
   press Bank. Now it's real GOLD in your wallet, and nothing can take it.

## The world has moods

- **Bright** — the Keeper is minting. New gold is being scattered as chests
  across the cove, the town, the highlands and the dungeon floors, on top of
  what the Hoard puts out. The richest hunting there is.
- **Quiet** — nothing new is minted, but chests still fall: the Hoard puts back
  what the world swallowed and hides it again. Nothing bites while the world is
  Quiet, so this is the safest hunting you will get — and the time to talk,
  trade, craft and hatch eggs.
- **Dark** — the deep places wake. Chests still fall, but skeletons will bite
  at any gold you're carrying. Bank often, carry little, and head for the Crypt
  if you're feeling brave.

The mood is set by the Keeper, who watches the price of gold. Press K to see
what the Keeper is up to and when the next turn comes.

## Why gold appears, and why it sometimes doesn't

Gold in Delulu Canyon is meant to be worth one ICP. Nobody keeps a chest of ICP
behind it; instead the Keeper adjusts how much gold exists.

- When gold trades **above** one ICP, there isn't enough of it. The Keeper
  mints more — as much as the market can actually absorb, and no more — and
  hides it in chests across the world. The world turns Bright, and the finders
  are paid.
- When gold trades **below** one ICP, there's too much. The Keeper mints
  nothing, the world turns Dark, and the Crypt opens: bury gold now, get a
  tombstone, and dig it up for gold plus a bonus once the price is back above
  one.

Either way the floors stay stocked, because the Hoard — every chest nobody
found, every body nobody reclaimed, every bite taken in the dark — sends the
world's own losses back out as chests, in every mood. Chests get bigger the
deeper you go: a chest on the deep floors is worth about twenty surface chests,
which is what the guards and the locked doors down there are for.

## Health, and what happens when it runs out

You have health, not a timer. Steps are free. Skeletons nibble, cows only kick
if you kick first, and dragons — which come once in a long while, always out in
the open — hit like a landslide. Standing on a vault or a town square heals you
to full.

If you fall, you become a **ghost**. Your body stays where you fell, holding
your gold. Walk back to it as a ghost — nothing can hurt you on the way — and
reclaim it. Take too long and your body returns to the earth, and your gold to
the Keeper's Hoard. Banked gold is never touched.

## Getting stronger

- **Items** hide in wooden chests across the world and drop from monsters: a
  cutlass, a dagger, potions of every size, a buckler, a lantern that shows you
  chests from farther away, a cloak that lets you walk past monsters untouched.
  Press I for your pack.
- **Torches.** Bind torches to you at the Masonry in Lanternfall. Every bound
  torch adds 10 HP (up to two hundred count), and bound torches open the deeper
  floors — the Drowned Chapel, the Bone Halls, the Deep Vault — where the
  chests are richer. Binding is not spending: they stay yours.
- **Bone dust** from every monster you beat brews into potions and pet food at
  a cauldron — there is one in town and another in Lanternfall.

## Torches, and where they come from

Torches are earned at the **Graveyard**: lend gold and ICP to the town's market
— a liquidity position in the pools outside the world — and you're paid in
torches over time. The people who keep the market honest end up owning the most
of the world.

Beside the Crypt, Brannock tends the plots. Bring a position and **plant** it:
planted, it grows torches for you around the clock, and its own trading fees
keep growing too and stay yours. **Uproot** whenever you like and the position
walks straight back to you; **claim** what has grown (it arrives over a week).
The game never holds your tokens: the position is minted to you, stays yours,
and uprooting hands it straight back.

## When the world goes Dark: the Crypt

From the outer lane east of town, the Procession Way runs under a lychgate into
Hallowmere: dead trees, lanterns, a bell-shrine that rings when the Keeper turns
Dark. The Crypt is at its end, and it only opens when the world is Dark.

Bring gold, bury it, and you'll be given tombstones — one for each gold. When
the light returns, dig them up for gold plus a bonus, paid from the Keeper's
Hoard. Every chest nobody found and every body nobody reclaimed goes into that
Hoard. The patient are paid by the careless.

## Eggs, chickens, and the Store

Eggs hide everywhere, including underground. Carry them and they hatch into
chickens that follow you — up to ten. An Egg Bagg from the Store plus some bone
dust warms an egg along. Feed your flock and they'll heal you a little faster,
and a well-fed hen sometimes lays.

Old Bessany's Store in Luméira sells egg baggs, an iron dagger, food, Marrow's
Compass, a bone axe, and one sword priced for legends. Two other counters keep
shop: Sable Ironhand at the forge in Lanternfall, who sells edges and not one
biscuit, and Maudy Ashgrave's pub in the Ossuary, which has hardtack, salt cod
and honeycake and nothing else.

## What things cost

One gold is meant to be worth about one ICP, so prices are small numbers with
several decimal places rather than round ones. The shop shows what everything
costs today; the realm can retune the list, so this guide names the shape of it
rather than the figures.

Food is the cheapest thing on any shelf — a biscuit, salt cod, a honeycake — and
a seed cake feeds your whole flock. Weapons climb steeply, and they climb in the
order they hit: the iron dagger is the bottom rung and the elvish longsword the
top, with a bone axe, a hunter's spear, an ashen blade and a wyrmtooth sabre
between them. Only the first two are on Bessany's shelf — the rest are Sable
Ironhand's, at the forge in Lanternfall. Gabriel's Sword sits far above all of
it, deliberately: it is a legend with a price to match, not a purchase anyone
plans.

Mending costs gold and materials — bone dust for the dagger, a dragon's scale
for the sword. And every second purchase burns half its price for good, which is
the Store doing its part for the peg.

## The strip at the top: the epoch chip

The Keeper turns every six hours. The strip at the top of your screen shows its
mood, the epoch number and a countdown; click ▸ to shrink it to a glowing dot.
When it turns, the bell rings and the strip names where the gold fell; the map
marks those zones with "✦ chests fell" for the rest of the epoch. A soft ring
under your feet brightens when an unopened chest is near. ☰ → Display turns any
of these off.

## Your welcome pouch, and the sponsors' gifts

After you accept the Terms at the gate, a small chest waits a few tiles from
where you land: half a gold, yours once you carry it to the vault. Not all
chests come from the Keeper — guilds and friends of the Canyon fill a treasury,
and their tokens fall as **sponsor chests** even when the world is Quiet. Open
one and you carry a **pouch** (gold, torches, or a sponsor's own coin); it is at
risk like gold, and it goes to your wallet when you **bank pouches** at the
vault. The Keeper's Board says who gave and where.

## Crates from other players

Since 0.17.0, chests can come from anyone. At the **Bazaar** a player can seal
any token they hold — GOLD, TORCH, or anything imported by its ledger canister
id — into a **crate**, carry it into the world, drop it for whoever finds it,
or hand it to a neighbour. Whoever opens it is paid exactly what the crate
says; the value itself never leaves the Bazaar's books while the crate travels.

Three things to know before you trust one:

- **Read the id, not the name.** Token names are not unique — anyone can deploy
  a token called anything — so the Bazaar shows the ledger canister id wherever
  a token is named, and tokens no operator has vouched for are marked
  **unvetted**. The id is the only honest name.
- **Crates fall with you.** Die carrying one and it lies with your body, for
  anyone who walks past.
- **A crate that rots unopened refunds its maker** — the tokens go home, less
  the seal fee.

## The beasts of the land

Sheep, goats, deer and boar graze the meadows and highlands; chickens scratch
by the Farmsteads, and ravens pick over the meadows, the highlands and the
graveyard. Goats and boars fight back if hit (a honeycake calms them); boars
gore like cows. In the shallow halls are rats, bats and giant spiders — a
spider's venom bites twice more after the hit — and wisps drift in the Grove,
harmless and hard to catch. Two floors down the company changes: cave crawlers
that take a lot of killing and leave venom behind, ash hounds that hit as hard
as a boar, gloom moths, and more bats. They drop hide, feathers, fangs and
wisp-light; at a cauldron, four hide and ten dust make a Hide Cloak (beasts do
half), three wisp-light and a feather a Wisp Lantern (you see farther). In the
deep floors the dark elf wizards bolt from three tiles away — an Ashen Cloak,
brewed from their thread, halves it.

## Trading with a neighbour

Stand next to another player and open a trade: each side lays out items, carried
gold or pouches; any change clears both Accepts; when both accept, the swap
happens at once. Relics cannot be traded.

For tokens you have already banked, the **Bazaar** trades at a distance: place
tokens with it, offer some for others, and take an offer somebody else has left
standing — they do not have to be online, or nearby, or even awake. A trade
happens completely or not at all, and the Bazaar publishes its own books.

## Where to go, and when

Read the strip at the top of your screen. Its colour is the Keeper's mood, and
the mood decides what is worth doing for the next six hours. The
[field guide](field-guide.md) is the full version of this; the short one:

### ☀ Bright — gold is falling

| do this | where |
|---|---|
| **Hunt the named zones** — the Keeper adds new gold on top of what the Hoard puts out, and the deeper the floor, the fatter the chest | wherever the chip says |
| **Bank what you carry** — carried gold is not yours until you do | the vault on Luméira's plaza, and one in every zone near the way in |
| **Dig up your tombstones** — the Hoard pays them back, with a bonus when gold is dear | the Crypt, Hallowmere |
| Spend, if you were going to | Old Bessany's Store — every second purchase burns half its price, which helps the peg |

### ○ Quiet — the Hoard stocks the floors

| do this | where |
|---|---|
| **Hunt anyway** — chests still fall, and nothing bites | the zones from the last Bright turn, and wherever the Hoard hid its share |
| **Craft** dust into potions, hide into cloaks, wisp-light into lanterns | any cauldron |
| **Bind torches** for health and the deep doors | the Masonry in Lanternfall |
| **Plant or uproot** a position, claim what has grown | Brannock's plots, Hallowmere |
| **Feed the flock, hatch eggs, repair, run errands** | the Farmsteads, the Store, the Keeper's Board |
| **Fish, ferry, barter** | the Harbour, and the counters in town |

### ☾ Dark — the Crypt opens

| do this | where |
|---|---|
| **Carry less, bank sooner** — a bite is a twentieth of what you carry | the vault, before you go down |
| **Bury gold for tombstones**, one for one — you are buying the recovery | the Crypt, Hallowmere, down the Procession Way |
| **Stay out of the deep** unless you are hunting monsters rather than gold | town, the Farmsteads, the guilds |
| **Take the ferry, rest at the inn** — small fares, and the inn mends you faster afterwards | Old Corliss at the jetty; the Lantern Inn |

### Always true

- **Chests fall every epoch, in every mood.** What the mood changes is how much
  new gold the Keeper puts in on top of the Hoard's, and how expensive it is to
  carry what you find.
- **Four epochs is a day.** An unfound chest, or your body with gold on it,
  crumbles to the Hoard after four. If you fell, get back within a day.
- **Unbinding takes six epochs**, so bind torches before you need them, not when.
- **The doors want torches bound, not spent:** ten for the Drowned Chapel, fifty
  for the Bone Halls, two hundred for the Deep Vault. Beyond two hundred they
  open doors but add no more health.
- **Curios** come out of wooden chests below ground — never on the surface, and
  more often the deeper you are — and once in a while out of a bottle on a
  fishing line. Quill Amberly at the Luméira inn pays bone dust for them, and
  pays better for a whole set.
- **The Ossuary**, a room of stacked bone under the Crypt, opens only to someone
  holding a tombstone. Maudy Ashgrave keeps a fire and a table down there.
- **The Obelisk on the plaza** glows with the same mood as the chip, if you
  would rather read the sky than a bar.

## Two worlds, and the gate between

Southwest of town, south of the harbour, a portal stands between two fountains
with a sign that tells you what it is. Through it is **Waterworld**: isles,
bridges, shallows a boat crosses, six dungeons of its own — a second world
with the same rules and the same money, running as its own canister.

Three things to know before you sail:

- **Bank first.** Carried gold and unbanked pouches do not travel. The gate
  will refuse you, politely, until they are in a vault — banked gold is yours
  in both worlds.
- **Everything else goes with you.** Gear, pets, titles, quests, curios,
  crates — the whole character steps through, and steps back the same way.
- **There is never two of you.** Mid-journey, the world you left holds your
  place; you arrive whole or you stay put.

## The guild halls

Two great halls stand open — the Sneed Guild Hall and the Tendys Hall — each a
nave two hundred tiles deep with side rooms, hearths, and an altar carrying
the guild's own mark, read live from its SNS. Each hall keeps four
**dispensers**: chests that never empty and never race. Each rolls its own
table for you, once an epoch — potions, cutlasses, the wider table, and,
rarely, the guild's **standard**, a flag carried as a staff that calls
lightning at fifteen tiles. Its short-armed cousin, the **Tideflame Censer**,
is brewed, not dispensed — see the Four Tides in the
[Quest Guide](quests.md).

## The loop

Find gold → bank it → bind torches to reach the richer floors, or plant gold
and ICP in the Graveyard to earn torches, or wait for the Dark and bury gold for
tombstones. Every gold you bank, every torch you earn, every tombstone you hold
is a real token in your wallet. The dungeon is just the fair way of deciding who
gets the new gold: the people who showed up and went looking.

Press H any time for help, J for your quests, L for the leaderboard, M for the
map.
